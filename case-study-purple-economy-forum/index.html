Case Study — Climate & Culture · Official COP22 Event

1st African Purple Economy Forum

“Climate threats, cultural responses” — on the eve of COP22, at La Mamounia.

La Mamounia, Marrakech — November 4–5, 2016

Placed just before the opening of COP22 and the entry into force of the Paris Agreement, the 1st African Purple Economy Forum brought business leaders, young professionals, elected officials, academics, media and civil society to Marrakech to ask how culture can make the response to climate change more human, more attractive and better adapted to each context. Held under the High Patronage of His Majesty King Mohammed VI and the patronage of UNESCO, and officially labelled by COP22, the forum was coordinated by Diversum, with Global Premium Events on the ground at La Mamounia.

Participants of the 1st African Purple Economy Forum — La Mamounia, Marrakech, 2016
Officially Labelled COP22

The forum was officially labelled as a COP22 event — part of the programme around the UN Climate Change Conference (COP22 / CMP12 / CMA1) hosted by Morocco in Marrakech in November 2016.

It was opened by Hakima El Haite, Moroccan Minister of the Environment, designated by His Majesty the King to represent him, and placed government representatives in direct dialogue with cultural figures, SME founders and multinational leaders.

The Venue

La Mamounia, Marrakech

The forum took place at La Mamounia, the historic palace hotel of Marrakech, set in its centuries-old gardens inside the city walls. Debates were held in the hotel’s salons, and participants met between sessions over lunch in the gardens.
