Case Study — Climate & Culture

1st African Purple Economy Forum

“Climate threats, cultural responses”

La Mamounia, Marrakech — November 4–5, 2016

On the eve of COP22 and the entry into force of the Paris Agreement, business leaders, young professionals, elected officials, academics, media and civil society met at La Mamounia to explore how culture can make the response to climate change more human, more attractive and better adapted to each context. The forum was coordinated by Diversum, with Global Premium Events delivering on the ground in Marrakech.

The forum was opened by Hakima El Haite, Moroccan Minister of the Environment, designated by His Majesty the King to represent him.

The Forum

Why culture belongs in the climate answer

The forum was deliberately placed before the opening of COP22. Its conviction: facing the planet’s ecological challenges, technology alone is not enough — only a response that also mobilises culture offers solutions that people adopt.

It followed a call published in Le Monde in July 2015 by 25 business leaders, researchers and artists, and two earlier international forums in Paris (2011) and Abu Dhabi (2013).

The Venue

La Mamounia, Marrakech

The forum was held at La Mamounia, Marrakech’s legendary palace hotel, set in its centuries-old gardens inside the city walls — a setting that carried the forum’s message that heritage and culture are part of a sustainable future.

Debates took place in the hotel’s salons; between sessions, participants met over lunch on the garden terrace.

Our Commitment

Engaged for the climate, event after event

Since COP22 in Marrakech, climate has been part of how we work. Every event has a footprint; our job is to make it smaller without making the experience poorer. Whenever our clients approve, we apply these practices:

Recycled & certified paper

Programmes, badges and printed materials on recycled or FSC-certified paper, printed in the quantities really needed.

Digital first

E-invitations, online registration, QR-code programmes and digital badges to reduce printing.

Reusable stands & signage

Modular stand structures and undated signage designed to be used again, event after event.

Local & seasonal catering

Local suppliers and seasonal menus, accurate guest counts, and surplus food redistributed where possible.

Less plastic

Water fountains and glass bottles instead of single-use plastic, and no plastic giveaways.

Shared transport

Group shuttles for delegations and venues chosen for easy access, to cut individual trips.
