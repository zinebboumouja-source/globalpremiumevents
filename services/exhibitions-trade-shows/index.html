Booth Design & Construction

See your stand in 3D before it’s built

Every stand starts as a photorealistic 3D concept, designed in-house around your brand, your space and the meetings you want to hold. You validate the design, then our team fabricates and installs exactly what you approved.

  • 01Custom architecture: curved frames, LED edge lighting, branded wall panels
  • 02Reception counter, storage and AV integration (screens, lighting)
  • 03Meeting and lounge areas sized for B2B conversations
  • 04Your logo and graphics applied on every surface before fabrication
01 Booking & Organizer Coordination
  • Space selection and booking (floor plan analysis, high-traffic zones)
  • Negotiation with show organizers
  • Administrative formalities (badges, exhibitor accreditation)
  • Coordination of mandatory deadlines (technical regulations, as-built documentation)
02 Design & Scenography
  • Custom scenographic design (moodboards, 2D/3D plans, photorealistic renders)
  • Stand architecture (modular, custom-built, two-level)
  • Art direction aligned with brand identity
  • Selection of materials and finishes (wood, metal, tension fabric, LED)
03 Fabrication & Installation
  • Workshop fabrication (wood/metal structures, large-format graphic cladding)
  • On-site build-up and teardown with a dedicated technical crew
  • Transport logistics (import/export, customs clearance for GCC/U.S. zones)
  • Technical installation: stage lighting, sound systems, LED screens, video mapping
04 Communication & Content
  • Pre-show communication strategy (teasers, targeted invitations)
  • Production of visual assets (roll-ups, POS displays, brochures, motion design)
  • On-site communication (announcements, activations, live social media)
  • Press relations where media coverage is required
05 Videography & Filming
  • Video coverage of the stand and key moments (interviews, product demonstrations)
  • Aftermovie editing for post-event promotion
  • Live streaming for on-stand conferences or product launches
06 Hostesses & Staffing
  • Selection and briefing of multilingual hosts and hostesses
  • Technical staff (stage managers, sound and lighting operators)
  • Interpreter coordination for international delegations
07 Corporate Goodies & Gadgets
  • Sourcing and customization of promotional items (branding, packaging)
  • Production management and on-site delivery
  • Selection tailored to the target market (culture, local regulations)
