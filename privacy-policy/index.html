Privacy Policy & Legal Notice

How we handle the information you share with us, and who is responsible for this website. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Global Premium Events (“GPE”, “we”, “us”), an international events and missions consultancy headquartered in Rabat, Morocco. For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at info@globalpremiumevents.com or +212 653512995.

2. What information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through our contact forms or by email: your name, email address, phone number, organization, the service or mission you are interested in and the content of your message. When you apply for a job, we also receive your CV. When you register for a B2B mission, we collect the information needed to organize your participation (company profile, participants, meeting preferences). We do not collect sensitive data such as health information through this website. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell or rent personal data.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal;
  • To manage our professional relationship with you if we work together;
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

4. Who receives it

Your information is used by the GPE team only. Messages sent through our website forms are transmitted by a form-delivery service (FormSubmit) to our mailbox. The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers only process data to deliver the service. As FormSubmit and GitHub are based in the United States, the information you send through our forms is transmitted outside Morocco for this purpose only.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 24 months. Data relating to clients and partners is kept for the duration of our relationship and for the period required by law.

6. Your rights

In accordance with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, and with the GDPR for visitors in the European Union, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to info@globalpremiumevents.com. You may also contact the Moroccan data-protection authority, the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record technical data such as your IP address as part of delivering the font files.
