Who We Are

Built in Africa. Connected to the world.

A Morocco-based international events and business-mission company, working at the intersection of events, market development and strategic connectivity — creating pathways into international markets for African organizations, and meaningful pathways into Africa for the world.

For twelve years, our in-house team has planned and delivered trade show participations, official missions and delegations, and corporate productions across Africa, Europe, the Gulf and the United States — carrying every project from first brief through on-the-ground execution to final report, under one accountable roof.

Leadership

Led from the front.

Sarah Zineb Boumouja is the Co-Founder and CEO of Global Premium Events — leading the organization for more than twelve years across trade shows, missions and corporate productions in Africa, Europe, the GCC and the United States.

She led the Organizing Committee behind the Africa Trade & Investment Global Summit in Washington, D.C., and in 2025 brought GPE's own accessibility challenges back to her alma mater, Indiana University's Kelley School of Business — a partnership that became Global Inclusive Events.

“Certain voices remained unheard — women, young individuals from rural areas, and students with disabilities were consistently missing from the transformative circle.”
