Case Study — Missions & Delegations

Zimbabwe at the Sharjah FDI Forum

One official investment mission, from visas to the stand to the investor table.

Sharjah, United Arab Emirates — December 9–11, 2018

Global Premium Events facilitated Zimbabwe’s official investment mission to the Sharjah FDI Forum 2018, hosted by the Sharjah FDI Office of the Sharjah Investment and Development Authority (Shurooq). We handled the delegation’s visas and travel, designed, built and staffed the Zimbabwe stand, matched the delegation with investors, and brought them into the pre-conference workshop on attracting foreign direct investment.

H.H. the Ruler of Sharjah tours the forum exhibition — Sharjah FDI Forum 2018
High-Level Recognition

On the opening day, His Highness Sheikh Dr. Sultan bin Muhammad Al Qasimi, Member of the Supreme Council and Ruler of Sharjah, visited the Zimbabwe stand with H.E. Sultan Bin Saeed Al Mansoori, UAE Minister of Economy, and H.E. Marwan Bin Jassim Al Sarkal, Executive Chairman of Shurooq. Sarah Zineb Boumouja, CEO of Global Premium Events, received His Highness, presented the official Zimbabwe delegation and walked him through the country’s investment opportunities. The delegation was then received in a meeting at the Majlis of His Highness the Ruler of Sharjah.

Sarah Zineb Boumouja presents the official Zimbabwe delegation to H.H. the Ruler of Sharjah — Zimbabwe stand, December 10, 2018

Days, one mission
A pre-conference workshop and a two-day forum with ministers, ambassadors, investment agencies and investors from Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas — with Zimbabwe present at every stage, from the workshop room to the exhibition floor and the main stage.

The program the delegation took part in

December 9 · Pre-conference workshop, Centro Sharjah

  • — Foreign Direct Investment workshop: integrating innovative solutions to boost competitiveness
  • — Current investment trends and using technology to detect opportunities — Bureau van Dijk
  • — Improving the investment climate and competitiveness in FDI promotion — Islamic Development Bank
  • — Branding locations and creating added value — M&C Saatchi
  • — Working with partners and building strategic relationships — KPMG
  • — Panel discussion with Q&A

December 10–11 · Sharjah FDI Forum

  • — Main address by H.E. Sultan Bin Saeed Al Mansoori, UAE Minister of Economy
  • — Recognition Awards by H.H. Sheikh Dr. Sultan bin Muhammad Al Qasimi
  • 6 panels: Global FDI, the future of chambers of commerce, the race for 5G, AI and investment, women advancement in business, sustainability beyond CSR
  • — Youth Circles side session on youth and the future of economies
  • — One-on-one interviews, moderated by CNBC Arabia
  • — Exhibition floor with national and institutional stands

Objectives

The mission had three objectives:

  • 01 Present Zimbabwe’s investment opportunities to Gulf and international investors
  • 02 Connect the delegation with investors, Sharjah’s economic institutions and its leadership
  • 03 Strengthen the delegation’s tools for promoting and attracting FDI
