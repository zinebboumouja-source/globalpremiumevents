Africa Trade & Investment Global Summit

Three days. 92 countries. One summit that opened doors across Africa.

World Trade Center · Ronald Reagan Building, Washington, D.C. — June 24–26, 2018

Global Premium Events' CEO, Sarah Zineb Boumouja, served as Head of the Organizing Committee for the Africa Trade and Investment Global Summit (ATIGS), with GPE as organizing partner — gathering 2,345 delegates over three days: key economic decision-makers from more than 70 countries, government delegations, African heads of delegation and business leaders, project developers and international investors.

Ribbon-cutting ceremony — World Trade Center, Washington, D.C.
High-Level Recognition

The summit was officially welcomed by the Mayor of Washington, D.C., who issued the welcome letter shown here, and brought together senior voices from government, finance and international institutions over the three-day program.

Official welcome letter — Mayor Muriel Bowser, Washington, D.C., June 24, 2018
