Vision & positioning

Who takes care of those who lead?

A first in MoroccoBridging business and wellbeingAn annual gathering

According to the World Health Organization, 15% of working-age adults were living with a mental disorder in 2019. Depression and anxiety lead to an estimated 12 billion working days lost every year, costing nearly one trillion US dollars.[1]

Yet leaders, whose decisions affect entire teams, rarely talk about it. On International Men's Day, Leading Well opens this conversation with leaders, employers and experts.
