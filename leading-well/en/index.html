Why this webinar

Leaders are the ones who talk least about their mental health.

On International Men's Day, whose official 2026 theme is "The Future Is Everyone", Global Premium Events brings together business leaders, employers, doctors and experts for an honest conversation: how to take care of yourself in order to lead others well, and what companies can do in practice. One of the first gatherings dedicated to this day in Morocco.
