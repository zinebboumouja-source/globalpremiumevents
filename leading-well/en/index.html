01

Context and rationale

Men's mental health, a public health and leadership issue

According to the World Health Organization, 15% of working-age adults were living with a mental disorder in 2019. Depression and anxiety lead to an estimated 12 billion working days lost every year, costing the global economy nearly one trillion US dollars.[1]

In this context, the role of leaders deserves particular attention: their decisions affect entire teams, and their own relationship with mental health shapes the culture of their organisations. On the occasion of International Men's Day, Leading Well opens this conversation with leaders, employers and experts.

This initiative is aligned with the United Nations 2030 Agenda and contributes to target 3.4 of Sustainable Development Goal 3: promoting mental health and well-being.[2]

SDG 3 · Target 3.4

“By 2030, reduce by one third premature mortality from non-communicable diseases through prevention and treatment and promote mental health and well-being.”
