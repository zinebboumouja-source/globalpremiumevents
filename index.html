About Global Premium Events

Global Premium Events (GPE) is a 100% women-owned international events and missions consultancy headquartered in Rabat, Morocco. For twelve years, our in-house team has structured trade show participations, official missions and delegations, and corporate productions for companies, chambers of commerce and government institutions — coordinating logistics, protocol, design and production under one roof.

Our work has carried the High Patronage of His Majesty King Mohammed VI and partnered with UNESCO on COP22 / CMP12 / CMA1, the UN Climate Change Conference held in Marrakech. In 2018, GPE was organizing partner to the Africa Trade & Investment Global Summit in Washington, D.C. — with CEO Sarah Zineb Boumouja serving as Head of the Organizing Committee — gathering 2,345 delegates from 92 countries.

Speaker · Moderator · Convener

Sarah Zineb Boumouja

Moroccan entrepreneur, international event strategist and panel moderator, Sarah Zineb Boumouja is the Co-Founder and CEO of Global Premium Events, a 100% women-owned company headquartered in Rabat that designs official missions, investment forums and summits between Africa, the Gulf, Europe and the United States.

Her education spans economics, business and law: she studied economics, is an alumna of Indiana University’s Kelley School of Business, and holds a master’s degree in Administrative Law and Public Policy. She also studied Korean language and literature, out of a lifelong interest in cultural diplomacy and in how culture opens doors that negotiation alone cannot.

Her master’s thesis, “Fiscal Governance and Regional Development in Morocco’s Southern Provinces: Toward a Differentiated Tax System under the 2007 Autonomy Initiative in Light of International Benchmarks”, earned High Honors at Abdelmalek Essaâdi University, and the jury recommended it for publication as the first study of its kind in Morocco. Her research also covers Moroccan sports diplomacy as a lever of soft power.

That legal and policy training shapes how she works. She doesn’t only fill a room: she reads the institutions, protocols and policy frameworks behind it, which is why ministries, investment agencies and official delegations trust her with their most visible moments.

Her career has been built on the ground. In Washington, D.C., she chaired the organizing committee of the Africa Trade & Investment Global Summit, 2,345 delegates from 92 countries, and was interviewed by Alhurra TV. In Sharjah, she personally presented Zimbabwe’s official delegation to His Highness the Ruler at the stand her team built. In Addis Ababa, she moderated the session with the African Union, ministers of health and Africa’s healthcare federations, and accompanied French- and Arabic-speaking delegations to their investor meetings. In Marrakech, she was on site at La Mamounia for the 1st African Purple Economy Forum, an officially labelled COP22 event under the High Patronage of His Majesty King Mohammed VI.

A voice for women’s leadership in Africa and for events that leave no one out, she returned to Indiana University to mentor Kelley School of Business students through the Global CLIP program, work that grew into Global Inclusive Events. Under her leadership, Global Premium Events was named “Best Global Event Management Company – North Africa” by MEA Markets at the African Excellence Awards 2022.

She speaks and moderates in English, French and Arabic.
